# Principle

A winning position is not a won game. The usual way to throw away an advantage is to hurry: grab more material,
allow counterplay, and let the opponent back in. The technique is the opposite.

# Technique

1. **Ask what your opponent wants.** Identify their only active ideas and take them away first.
2. **Trade pieces, not pawns,** when ahead in material. Fewer pieces means less counterplay; pawns are your winning
   potential.
3. **Improve your worst piece** before starting decisive action.
4. **Keep your king safe.** Many reversals start with a check you didn't consider.
5. **Don't calculate more than you need.** When a simple safe move keeps a large advantage, play it.

# Common failure modes

* Grabbing a pawn and allowing a strong attack or a passed pawn.
* Relaxing after a gain of material and missing the opponent's only tactic.
