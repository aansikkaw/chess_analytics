# Principle

Forced mates are found by recognition more than by calculation. When the enemy king is short of squares, check
whether a known pattern fits before calculating anything else.

# Patterns worth knowing by name

* **Back-rank mate**: a king trapped behind its own pawns.
* **Smothered mate**: a knight mating a king surrounded by its own pieces.
* **Arabian mate**: rook and knight cooperating near the corner.
* **Anastasia's mate**: knight and rook (or queen) trapping the king against the edge.
* **Boden's mate**: two bishops on crossing diagonals.
* **Epaulette mate**: a king blocked on both sides by its own pieces.

# Habit

When your opponent's king has two or fewer free squares, look at every check before any other move.
