# Principle

Most worse positions are not lost yet. The opponent still has to convert, and many players handle that badly.
Your job is to make it hard.

# Practical defence

* **Stay calm and keep calculating.** Collapses usually come from a single despairing move.
* **Look for active counterplay** before passive waiting; a passive position with no prospects is the hardest to hold.
* **Know drawing resources:** opposite-coloured bishops, rook endgames a pawn down, fortresses.
* **Trade the right pieces:** the attacker's most dangerous piece first.
* **Use the clock:** present problems that cost your opponent time.
