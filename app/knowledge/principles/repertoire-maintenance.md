# Three questions to ask of any repertoire

1. **Do I remember it?** If you keep deviating from your own preparation, the file is not doing its job. Drill the
   positions you forget, starting with the ones you have already faced in games.
2. **Does it cover what opponents actually play?** Gaps where opponents leave your preparation early matter more
   than deep sidelines nobody plays. Fill the most frequent gaps first.
3. **Do I understand where the lines end?** Every prepared line ends somewhere; you should know the plans there,
   and the engine should not consider the end position bad for you.

# Maintenance routine

* After each tournament, compare your games against your preparation.
* Add the opponent moves you met that weren't covered.
* Re-check lines whose final evaluation looks poor.
