# Principle

A plan comes from the position, not from wishful thinking. List the **imbalances** and play toward the ones that
favour you.[^silman-reassess]

# Imbalances to check

* Pawn structure: weaknesses, passed pawns, pawn majorities, open files.
* Minor pieces: good versus bad bishop, knight outposts.
* Space and piece activity.
* King safety on both sides.
* Development and initiative.

# From imbalance to plan

* Identify your **worst piece** and improve it.
* Choose a **pawn break** that opens lines where you are stronger.
* Trade pieces that defend your opponent's weaknesses.
