# Principle

Mistake rates climb sharply when the clock runs low, regardless of playing strength. The goal is not to play fast,
but to spend time where it matters and never be forced to make critical decisions on reflex.

# Practical rules

* **Reserve rule:** keep a fixed fraction of your starting time (for example 20%) in hand until the time control or
  move 30. Treat crossing it as an alarm.
* **Spend on transitions:** trades, pawn breaks, moving from opening to middlegame, entering an endgame. These moments
  decide games; routine recaptures don't.
* **Use known positions to bank time:** in opening lines you have prepared, move quickly and save the minutes.
* **Decide, then verify once:** after choosing a move, do one blunder check, then play it. Re-calculating the
  same line three times is where most time disappears.

# Training

* Play longer time controls for a while if most losses come from time trouble.
* Review where your clock went in lost games, not just which moves were wrong.
