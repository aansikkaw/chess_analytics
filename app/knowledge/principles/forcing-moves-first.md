# Principle

Tactics happen through forcing moves: **checks, captures and threats**, in that order of urgency. Strong players scan
these candidates first, for themselves and for the opponent, before settling on a quiet plan.

# How to practise it

* At every move, list the checks, captures and threats available to you. It takes seconds and finds most shots.
* Then do the same from your opponent's side of the board.
* When a forcing move doesn't work immediately, ask what small change would make it work (a defender removed,
  a king on a worse square). That question is how combinations are found.

# Signs you're skipping this step

* You often find the winning move one move too late, when reviewing the game.
* Your losses include many "I didn't see that check".
