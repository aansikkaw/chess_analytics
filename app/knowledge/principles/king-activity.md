# Principle

In the middlegame the king hides; in the endgame it fights. With queens off and few pieces left, bring the king
toward the centre or toward the pawns that decide the game.

# Details

* Count king moves to the key squares in pawn endings; the race is often decided by one tempo.
* Opposition and key squares decide king-and-pawn endings; learn them before more complex endings.
* Don't over-protect the king out of habit when the mating danger is gone.
