# Principle

Opening mistakes at club level are rarely about forgetting move 18 of theory. They come from violating basic
principles once the opponent leaves your preparation.

# Principles

* **Develop pieces toward the centre**, knights usually before bishops, and avoid moving the same piece twice without
  a reason.
* **Fight for the centre** with pawns and pieces.
* **Castle early** unless there is a concrete reason not to, and don't open lines in front of your own king.
* **Don't grab material at the cost of development** unless you have checked the consequences concretely.
* **Know the plans, not just the moves:** for each line you play, know the typical pawn breaks and where the pieces
  belong.

# When your opponent leaves your preparation

Slow down. This is a high-value moment to spend time (see [Clock management](clock-management.md)).
