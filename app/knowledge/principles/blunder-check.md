# Why it matters

Most rating points below master level are lost to one-move oversights, not to deep strategic errors. A piece left
*en prise*, or a defender pulled away from its duty, decides more club games than any opening.

# The check (before you press the clock)

1. **What did my opponent's last move change?** New attacks, new lines opened, a piece that stopped defending.
2. **After my move, which of my pieces are loose?** A piece that is undefended, or defended fewer times than it is attacked.
3. **What are my opponent's checks, captures and threats** in the position after my move? Look at all of them, briefly.
4. **Does my move remove a defender?** Pieces often guard something invisible: a back rank, a key square, another piece.

# Habits that help

* Say "loose pieces drop off" (LPDO) to yourself in sharp positions.
* In time trouble, shrink the check to step 3 only. Never skip it entirely.
