How it works Plans

Find the part of your game that's costing you rating.

Enter your Lichess username. Stockfish checks your recent games and rates you skill by skill: openings, middlegame, endgames, tactics, time, converting and defending.

Free and no account needed. It takes about half a minute.

Four jobs, one place: your own games.

Game review explains one game. Plateau Breaker reads all of them, finds the habit that costs you the most, and keeps working on it with you.

Diagnose

A rating for each of 7 skills from every move you've played, and the habits behind your losses.

  • Rating DNA across hundreds of games
  • Every game on a board, mistakes marked
  • Openings and progress over time

Train

Your own mistakes come back as puzzles until you stop making them.

  • Spaced repetition: 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days
  • A 7-day plan built on your weakest skills

Prepare

Check your ChessBase repertoire against the games you actually played, and scout your next opponent.

  • Where you forgot your prep
  • Opponent moves your prep doesn't cover
  • Their weak lines and habits

Coach

Ask anything about your play. Every move and evaluation in an answer is checked against your games first.

  • Links to the exact positions
  • Anything unverified is flagged

Built for the night before round one.

Upload the repertoire you keep in ChessBase. We compare it with every game you've played and show where you left your own preparation, which replies it doesn't cover, and which lines end badly. Then we drill those positions.

Before a tournament, the Event Pass gives you Pro for 10 days to scout every opponent.
